**Summary of Common Facts, Points of Disagreement, and Source Quality Limitations**

**Common Facts (Across Multiple Sources)**

1.  **Release Date:** All post-June 29 articles agree that DeepSeek has officially stated V4 will launch in **mid-July 2026**.
2.  **Pricing:** Multiple, independent sources from June 29-30 confirm the introduction of an API **"峰谷定价" (peak/valley pricing)** mechanism.
3.  **Rumored Delays:** Sources from February to April consistently report that V4's launch was delayed multiple times from its initial rumored dates (Spring Festival, then March/April).
4.  **Domestic Chip Adaptation:** There is a consistent rumor (reported by TechWeb and others) that V4 will be deeply adapted to domestic chips (Huawei, Cambricon). Though this was not confirmed in the June official announcements.

**Points of Disagreement / Contradictions**

- **Release Timeline:** The major disagreement is between the Phase 1 rumors (which predicted a launch in early 2026, with many false starts in Feb/March/April) and the Phase 2 official confirmation (which finally sets the date for mid-July).
- **Chip Strategy:** The Phase 1 article from **松松技术笔记** stated that DeepSeek **did not** share V4 with Nvidia/AMD [Source 3], while the March article from **AI 宋一点** suggested an **Nvidia/Groq partnership** [Source 12], and **卧龙说** speculated on **domestic independence** [Source 15]. These are contradictory third-party interpretations. The Phase 2 articles do not directly address chip strategy in detail.
- **Multimodal Capabilities:** **TechWeb** reported V4 was expected to have **multimodal capabilities** [Source 13], but this specific term was not prominently featured in the Phase 2 official announcements.

**Source Quality Limitations**

1.  **Phase 1 Reliance on "Insider Sources":** The early rumors (February-April) are heavily based on "知情人士" (insider sources) cited by foreign media or loosely sourced "leaks." **AI 前哨营** [Source 1], **腾讯科技** [Source 5, 14], and others explicitly note that these are unconfirmed rumors. These sources lack direct confirmation from DeepSeek.
2.  **Low Authority of Some Accounts:** Many sources are from individual bloggers or niche accounts (e.g., **瑶瑶的精神世界** [Source 11], **梁非一人AI** [Source 4], **卧龙说** [Source 15]), which limits their authority as primary news sources. They are summaries of other reports.
3.  **Information Cascades in Phase 2:** The multiple articles from June 29-30 (**IT之家** [Source 6], **硅基觉醒中** [Source 7], **公众实时小助手** [Source 8], **AI 与日常** [Source 9], **永乐-明谋Ai** [Source 10]) appear to be reporting on the *same* official announcement. While **IT之家** [Source 6] is a reputable tech news site, the others are likely providing commentary on the same event, creating an echo chamber effect.
4.  **Lack of Official Verification in This Search:** The search results did not return a direct link to an official DeepSeek announcement or a press release from an undisputed primary source (e.g., DeepSeek's own official WeChat account or website). The **IT之家** [Source 6] article is the closest to a reliable secondary source. The original official source should be sought for final verification.
